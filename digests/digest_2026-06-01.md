### Employment effects of EU-ETS prices
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Authors** | Not specified |
| **Source** | ECB Working Papers — 26 May 2026 |
| **Link** | [URL](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3237~617a2b8de5.en.pdf) |
| **Research Question** | How do carbon prices under the EU Emissions Trading System affect employment across different job types? |
| **Data Used** | EU-ETS price data with employment statistics, focus on European labor markets, time period not specified |
| **Key Findings** | See bullets below |

- 1% increase in EU-ETS prices leads to 0.2% employment decline after 1.5 years
- Negative employment effects are strongest for polluting jobs, but also affect green jobs
- Free allowance allocation and employment protection legislation moderate the impact

**Tracker Relevance:** Direct evidence of how carbon pricing mechanisms affect labor markets during the energy transition, relevant for assessing social costs of climate policies.
