### The role of biodiversity risk in shaping bank lending decisions
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Authors** | Not specified |
| **Source** | ECB Working Papers — May 13, 2026 |
| **Link** | [https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3232~0420309406.en.pdf](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3232~0420309406.en.pdf) |
| **Research Question** | Do banks incorporate firm-level biodiversity risk into their lending decisions? |
| **Data Used** | Large sample of syndicated loans matched to firm-level biodiversity risk measures; novel text-based indicator from corporate disclosures. |
| **Key Findings** | See bullets below |

- Borrowers with higher biodiversity risk face significantly higher loan spreads
- Banks primarily adjust along pricing margin rather than restricting credit supply 
- Environmental violations increase the sensitivity of loan pricing to biodiversity risk

**Tracker Relevance:** Demonstrates how environmental risks beyond carbon are being priced into financial markets, expanding the scope of climate-related financial disclosures.
