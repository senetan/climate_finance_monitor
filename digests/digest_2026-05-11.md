### Physical climate risk, credit risk and lending activity
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Authors** | Not specified |
| **Source** | ECB Working Papers — 2026-05-06 |
| **Link** | [PDF](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3224~8735166667.en.pdf) |
| **Research Question** | How does physical climate risk shape bank lending activity and credit quality following major flood events? |
| **Data Used** | High-resolution Copernicus flood geospatial maps, loan-level AnaCredit data, four major European floods (2021-2024) |
| **Key Findings** | See bullets below |

- Lending increases 3.5-5% immediately after floods due to liquidity demand, followed by similar magnitude contraction next quarter
- Interest rates follow same pattern while default rates rise persistently by 0.7 percentage points
- Relationship banks extend 10 percentage points more credit to affected firms but impose tighter collateral requirements

**Tracker Relevance:** Directly relevant to physical risk measurement and how climate shocks translate into financial market impacts through credit channels.
